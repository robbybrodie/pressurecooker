Methodology and caveats
Work-pattern switch. Same effort applies both utilisation sliders unchanged across eras. Historical second-earner mode scales Person 2 utilisation by the model calibration: 1985 ×0.504, 1990 ×0.568, 2021 ×0.902, 2026 ×1.000. The resulting utilisation drives both Person 2 income and childcare hours.
House. A selected 2026 Sydney house price is translated backwards using each era's median Sydney house-price ratio, preserving the household's relative position to the median.
Mortgage. Fully amortising principal-and-interest payment. 0% deposit means full financing; 100% deposit means a cash purchase. LMI, stamp duty, conveyancing and transaction costs are not modelled.
School fees. The 2026 amount is translated backwards using an ABS Education CPI-based scaler. The 1985 point uses a lower-confidence bridge because the older CPI grouped recreation and education.
Childcare. Care demand is generated from overlapping paid work: 50 × max(0, Person 1 utilisation + effective Person 2 utilisation − 1), multiplied by the work-schedule-overlap factor and capped at 50 hours per child per week.
Interpretation. This is a transparent normalised household-pressure model, not a forecast and not a claim that every Sydney household had these exact circumstances.
